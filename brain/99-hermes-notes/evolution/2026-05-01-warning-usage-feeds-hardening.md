## Pitfalls Discovered

1. **Metric semantics must match actuator semantics.**
   - Hook routes are not provider-routed work. Penalizing them in route diversity is misleading.
   - The correct metric should measure diversity where multi-model routing is possible.

2. **Warning governance should compress durable memory, not delete facts blindly.**
   - The memory warning was real by byte count, but entries were mostly durable. Compression solved it without losing important lessons.

3. **Nightly skill audit score depends on explicit boundary/examples/tests.**
   - A skeletal skill can pass basic frontmatter checks but still remain low quality.
   - Adding concrete boundary conditions and test-prompts makes the audit result meaningful.

4. **Use targeted tests for metric policy changes.**
   - The `usage-score` change could otherwise silently regress back to counting hook bookkeeping rows.
